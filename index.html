Hi! My name is Josephine Ko, but I usually go by Josie. I am currently a student at the University of California, Riverside, where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and expect to graduate in June 2029. I have always enjoyed working with others, learning new things, and finding ways to make a positive impact on my community. Through my work experience, volunteering, and extracurricular activities, I have developed communication, leadership, and teamwork skills that I hope to continue improving throughout college and my future career. here

One of my most meaningful work experiences was being a Camp Leader for the City of Fremont Recreation Services during the summers of 2024 and 2025. I worked with children between the ages of 5 and 12, where I planned daily activities, helped lead lessons, and made sure everyone felt included and supported. Working with children taught me how important patience, responsibility, and communication are, especially when everyone has different personalities and needs. I also learned how to adapt to unexpected situations and work closely with other camp leaders to create a positive environment. Outside of work, volunteering has been a big part of my life. From 2023 to 2025, I volunteered with several organizations, including Heart in Motion, Friends of Children with Special Needs (FCSN), and Hands On Bay Area. At Heart in Motion, I helped organize running events by checking in participants, distributing supplies, and supporting runners throughout the events. At FCSN, I worked with children with special needs by helping them participate in activities such as arts and crafts, dancing, and games. This experience helped me become more understanding of others and showed me how even small actions can make a difference in someone's day. here

I also volunteered at local libraries and helped prepare and serve meals to individuals in need through Saint Vincent de Paul of Alameda County. These experiences allowed me to meet people from different backgrounds and better understand the importance of giving back to my community. I was also honored to receive the President's Volunteer Service Award Bronze Medal, which recognized my dedication to community service. Besides volunteering and academics, music and sports have played a major role in my life. I was a violinist in the East Bay Youth Orchestra from 2018 to 2024 and a varsity swimmer at American High School from 2021 to 2024. Being involved in these activities taught me discipline, time management, perseverance, and the importance of working toward goals. I especially enjoyed being part of a team where everyone's effort contributed to something bigger. As I continue my education at UC Riverside, I hope to gain more professional experience, explore opportunities related to psychology, and develop skills that will help me in my future career. I consider myself someone who is hardworking, open to learning, and willing to take on new challenges. My goal is to continue growing both personally and professionally while making a positive difference in the lives of others. here
